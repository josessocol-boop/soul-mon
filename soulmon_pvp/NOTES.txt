AI changes (2026-10-19), ai/ai_controller.gd:
- Keep-away bots no longer back into a wall: within 150 px of a wall they step toward the opponent instead (WALL_EDGE).
- Melee lead check: a melee move is only started if the gap at the middle of its active frames (predicted from how fast the opponent is closing) is inside its hitbox reach. Stops whiffs at a retreating opponent.
- Dodge: a dash away from an opponent melee move that is still in windup, checked after the bot's reaction time (reaction_time). Only moves with a windup longer than the reaction time can be dodged (most of the fast ones cannot). DODGE_CHANCE 0.7.

AI fixes for missed attacks (2026-10-19):
- _pick_down: when only the Down version of an attack can reach, it is always chosen. It used to pick the normal basic 10% of the time, and that whiffed every time.
- _drop_stale_presses: a buffered attack re-checks which version (normal or Down) reaches before it fires, so a press set to Down while the opponent was in range is switched if the opponent has since moved out (and the reverse).
- _variant_reaches: melee attacks are not started into a dashing or invincible opponent.
Measured on 176 matches per fighter: melee connects vs standing opponents went from 24% to about 83% for basics at range.

AI projectile and trap avoidance (2026-10-19), ai/ai_controller.gd _avoid_hazards():
- Stops walking toward an opponent's shot that is coming down the bot's lane (it was walking into them about a third of the time when a shot was in view).
- Dashes through such a shot once the reaction time has passed (dash i-frames), one roll per shot (PROJ_DODGE_CHANCE 0.85).
- Hops an opponent trap that is on the bot's path (TRAP_HOP_DIST 110), and dashes out of one it is already standing in (TRAP_AVOID_CHANCE 0.85 per trap).
Measured on the 6-fighter subset (Mewtwo, Tinkaton, Toxicroak, Decidueye, Lucario, Alakazam; 6 repeats, 180 matches), trap hits taken per game: Toxicroak 1.02 -> 0.52, Mewtwo 1.10 -> 0.77, Alakazam 0.17 -> 0.07, Tinkaton flat (0.68 -> 0.72).
Projectile hits taken per game did NOT drop (Mewtwo 13.3 -> 15.6, Tinkaton 12.7 -> 12.9, Lucario 9.3 -> 9.4). An instant, always-taken dodge gave the same numbers, so the gap is not the reaction time: most shots are first seen within 100 px (they spawn on the bot), or the bot is in hitstun or mid-attack, where no dash can start. Fixing that needs spacing changes, which move balance, so it was left alone.
